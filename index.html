OUR LOVE STORY ❤️ — Ray & Adepah

We met during our National Youth Camp All Night, and we had no idea that night would become the beginning of us.

Adepah was sitting in front, Ray was sitting right behind. During ministration, we were asked to hold someone and sing the song We Stand. Ray had no one beside him, so he reached out and held Adepah.

That one touch, that one moment — it changed everything.

We sang together, we smiled, we took pictures together, thinking it was just for that night. We didn't know we were creating our first memory.

After the service, Adepah came for the pictures and videos, and exchanged numbers, and just like that, a conversation started.

From requesting pictures to daily check-ups, from late-night calls and prayers together, from genuine friendship to falling deeply in love...

What started as Ray holding a stranger to sing We Stand has become Ray & Adepah learning to stand together for a lifetime.

Today, we are not just friends — we are fiancés, we are answered prayers to each other.

God truly writes the most beautiful love stories.
